# Kiroween demo: The Quiet Lantern Garden

## The 2-minute story

**0:00–0:15 — Frame the problem**
"Digital activities often assume one correct way to participate. Autween starts by letting the participant choose."

**0:15–0:35 — Choose a participation mode**
Open `/play/lantern`. Show Symbols, then Words. Explain that the same activity can be approached with visual symbols or written labels; neither is treated as the better option.

**0:35–1:05 — Change sensory presentation**
Open Sensory settings. Turn on quiet colours, reduce decorative motion, choose higher contrast, and switch visual space to Simple. Point out that each change is explicit and reversible. No camera, microphone, emotion inference or automatic adaptation is involved.

**1:05–1:30 — Create**
Add a lantern, moon and one other element. Remove one. Add an optional written note. Show the on-screen explanation that lists the exact settings selected.

**1:30–1:50 — Agency**
Pause the activity, resume it, then reset the scene. Skip the activity using the visible control. Emphasize that there is no score, timer or penalty for stopping.

**1:50–2:00 — Close**
"Autween doesn't try to work out what a child needs. It gives the participant clear choices, explains what those choices change, and lets them change their mind."

## Recording checklist

- Use synthetic demo content only. Never enter a real child's name or personal information.
- Start with a clean browser session at `/play/lantern`.
- Record at a readable desktop viewport; make a separate narrow/mobile pass.
- Capture keyboard focus on the participation controls and scene-piece buttons.
- Keep the activity silent; do not add jump scares, flashing lights or time pressure in editing.
- Show the actual running app. Do not use mockups as evidence of functionality.
- Do not describe the demo as clinically validated, therapeutic, or ready for unsupervised use.
