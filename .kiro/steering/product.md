# Autween Product Steering

## Mission
Build an adaptive social play and expression platform that lets autistic children connect, create, and participate on their own terms.

## Non-negotiable principles
- Adapt the environment; do not train children to appear neurotypical.
- Never optimize for eye contact, masking, speech frequency, compliance, or suppression of harmless stimming.
- Preserve child choice, pause, refusal, exit, and reset.
- Support different communication modes and asynchronous participation.
- Prefer shared-interest creation and play over compulsory conversation.
- Safety policy overrides personalization and recommendation logic.
- Use synthetic data only in prototypes and automated tests.
- Do not infer diagnosis, emotion, intent, intelligence, or social competence.
- No public child profiles, stranger messaging, or location sharing in the initial product.

## Engineering constraints
- Prefer a lightweight responsive web application for the first prototype.
- Avoid paid model/API dependencies.
- Prefer deterministic and explainable behaviour before ML.
- Keep privacy boundaries explicit in code and tests.
- Use accessible semantic UI and keyboard navigation.
- Do not claim clinical efficacy or production-grade safeguarding.

## Language
Use respectful, non-deficit language. Avoid describing autistic communication as broken, deficient, or something to normalize. Avoid claiming that all autistic children share the same preferences.
